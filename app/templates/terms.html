AI assistants can make mistakes. We do not control how an AI interprets instructions and are not responsible for actions it takes on your server. Review audit logs regularly.

Availability, restarts and technical behavior

We work to maintain high availability for ModLink, but acknowledge the dependencies on external services:

  • Service restarts: All credentials, connection configurations, and audit records persist in durable storage. System updates or restarts do not reset or invalidate your active connections.
  • Token lifecycles & expiration: Web portal session cookies expire after 30 days of inactivity. Authorization codes expire after 10 minutes. API keys remain valid until explicitly rotated or revoked by you.
  • External outages: If Discord APIs or our backend database become temporarily unavailable, ModLink fails closed and refuses moderation requests. Destructive operations will not execute in a disconnected or partially functional state.
  • Post-disconnection data: When a server disconnects or revokes ModLink, audit logs for that server are kept for 30 days for incident reviews before permanent removal.
