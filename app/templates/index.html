Questions

Which servers can I control?

Only servers where you have administrative access. ModLink checks this when you log in with Discord.

Where do I find my endpoint and token?

After you log in with Discord, ModLink gives you a private MCP endpoint and token for your servers.

Can I stop an AI from acting on my server?

Yes. Open the dashboard and select Revoke next to any connection.

Will the AI act without asking me?

Actions that need approval appear under Pending approvals in the dashboard, where you can approve or reject them before they expire.
