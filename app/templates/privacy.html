Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

ModLink lets you connect your Discord server to an AI assistant through a private, permission-scoped endpoint. This policy explains what data we handle to provide that service.

Information we collect

  • Discord account data: when you log in with Discord we receive your user ID, username, avatar, and the list of servers you belong to along with your permissions in them.
  • Connection data: the name, description, permission level, allowed servers and status of each AI connection you create, plus when it was last used.
  • Activity records: an audit log of actions taken through ModLink, including the tool used, server, target user ID, result and time, and any actions awaiting your approval.

How we use it

We use this information to verify that you administer a server, issue and enforce access tokens, carry out the moderation actions you authorize, show you the audit log, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your data.

Data shared with AI providers

When you connect an AI client such as Claude or ChatGPT, the information returned by moderation tools (for example member lists, messages or audit entries) is sent to that provider under your account. Their handling of that data is governed by their own policies.

Data retention periods & lifecycle

  • Audit records: Retained for 90 days from creation for compliance and dispute review, after which they are automatically pruned.
  • Session & login tokens: Web dashboard sessions expire after 30 days of inactivity. Expired session records are purged within 7 days.
  • API keys & connection secrets: Stored in encrypted form (AES-128-CBC + HMAC-SHA256 authenticated encryption) at rest. When you click "Revoke" or "Rotate Key" in the dashboard, the key is revoked immediately.
  • Audit records after server disconnection: When a server removes the bot or disconnects, audit records for that server are retained for 30 days to facilitate final audits, after which all corresponding logs are permanently purged.

Account & data deletion steps

You have full control over your data:

  1. Immediate self-service revocation: Click "Revoke" next to any connection in the ModLink Dashboard, or click "Rotate Key" to immediately invalidate old keys.
  2. Complete data erasure: To permanently erase all your account metadata, sessions, and server connection configs, email modlink.mcp.baddie@gmail.com with your Discord User ID. All associated data will be deleted within 14 business days.

Production behavior & service guarantees

  • Service restarts: All credentials, connection settings, and audit logs are persistently stored in a transactional database. Scheduled or unexpected service restarts do not destroy or reset your tokens; normal operation resumes immediately upon restart.
  • Token expiration: User session cookies expire after 30 days. OAuth authorization codes expire after 10 minutes. API keys remain active until rotated or revoked.
  • Discord & database unavailability: If Discord or our database is unreachable, ModLink fails closed. Moderation tools return explicit service availability errors; no partial or unrecorded destructive actions will ever be performed.

Security

Access tokens are scoped to the servers you administer, and every action is recorded. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please keep your tokens private and revoke any you no longer use.
